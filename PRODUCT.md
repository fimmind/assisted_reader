# Product

## Register

product

## Users

English learners reading long-form texts who want vocabulary help without leaving the reading flow. Their primary task is reading, with definitions available contextually when an unfamiliar word needs explanation.

## Product Purpose

Easeword is an assisted reader that estimates unfamiliar vocabulary, highlights useful words, and provides definitions without overwhelming the text. Success means readers can understand and continue reading with minimal interruption.

## Brand Personality

Calm, minimal, and reader-first. The experience should feel closer to Kindle, Readwise Reader, Apple Books, and Notion than to a gamified language-learning product.

## Anti-references

Avoid Duolingo-like gamification, bright reward screens, badges, mascots, cluttered dashboards, and assistance that competes with the text.

## Design Principles

- Keep reading as the primary activity.
- Reveal vocabulary assistance only when it is useful.
- Make contextual help easy to follow and dismiss.
- Preserve a quiet interface with minimal visual interruption.
- Reuse familiar interaction patterns consistently.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve the project's current accessibility conventions, including keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, semantic labels, and existing theme support.
